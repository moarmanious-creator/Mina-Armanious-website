Profile

Precision in the document. Judgement in the structure.

Mina Armanious is a banking, finance and venture capital lawyer whose career has been built inside the legal and credit functions of leading regional banks.

Today Mina leads alliance and contract work for Banque Misr's Innovation and Strategic Venture Sector and acts as legal counsel to the Board of BM Ventures, the bank's venture capital arm, running legal due diligence and negotiating term sheets, SAFEs, convertible notes and shareholders' agreements for technology investments across Egypt and MENA.

Before that, Mina headed the contracts and collaterals function for First Abu Dhabi Bank Misr across five jurisdictions, led an eight-person documentation team at Banque du Caire, and advised on legal risk across a USD 1.3bn corporate portfolio at Al Ahli Bank of Kuwait – Egypt. The result is a practitioner who understands both sides of the table: how lenders and investors assess risk, and how a document must be built to carry it.

Approach

How the work gets done

  1. 01

    Commercial first, then legal

    Understand the transaction, and what each party needs from it, before a word is drafted. Good documents follow good structure.

  2. 02

    Built for enforcement

    Security and covenants are tested on the day things go wrong, not the day they are signed. Every package is drafted with that day in mind.

  3. 03

    Clear, and on time

    Institutions run on deadlines. Clear advice, quick turnaround and no unnecessary back-and-forth: the qualities colleagues mention most.
