Practice

Across the full investment lifecycle

From the LP commitment to the portfolio restructuring: one counsel across every stage.

  1. 01

    Fund commitments

    Diligence on LPAs, subscription agreements and PPMs; side-letter negotiation; pre-signing conditions.

  2. 02

    Structuring

    Term sheets, SAFEs, convertible notes and valuation caps; holding structures across Delaware, the Netherlands, the BVI and the UAE.

  3. 03

    Due diligence

    Corporate structure, IP, regulatory and cap-table review, with risk weighed against investment strategy and appetite.

  4. 04

    Negotiation & closing

    SPAs and SHAs; investor protections; side letters to execution version.

  5. 05

    Portfolio & governance

    Every portfolio position reviewed case by case: matured-note extensions, valuation-cap amendments, restructurings, assignments and liquidation events; board memoranda and advice.

  6. 06

    External counsel & controls

    Legal-panel RFP, portfolio retainer and legal-spend analysis; bilingual SOPs for approvals.
