IProfile

Where law, capital and investment strategy meet

A venture capital and private equity lawyer with 6+ years across four leading banks in Egypt and the MENA region. Mina leads legal workstreams for VC and PE investments, strategic alliances and fund-level matters at Banque Misr's Innovation & Strategic Ventures Sector.

The foundation is credit-side: legal documentation for credit portfolios exceeding USD 7 billion across Egypt, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain and Saudi Arabia, spanning syndicated loans, structured and Islamic finance, investment banking products and complex collateral. Mina advises on investment structuring, legal due diligence, governance, transaction execution and legal risk.
